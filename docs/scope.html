Scope and conventions

The formal proof has explicit hypotheses. These conventions keep the manuscript, the guide, and Lean aligned.

What is proved

The physical known-spectrum optimum; one spectrum-independent cloner with compact-uniform fidelity and the regular compact-set unknown-spectrum minimax theorem; the literal projector-state minimax theorem; full-environment PCT limits and rank-bound comparisons and guarantees; the named representation, probabilistic, Gaussian, LAN, compression, and asymptotic statements linked throughout these guides. The full statement correspondence is recorded in formalization/PROOF_MAP.md.

Fidelity convention

F always means root fidelity, Tr sqrt(sqrt(ρ) σ sqrt(ρ)). Squared fidelity and squared infidelity are written explicitly as F² and 1−F². Physical dimension is denoted D here; many Lean statements use D=d+1, while projector statements use D=r+k. Compact-set optimality requires regular closure in the full trace-one affine hyperplane. Small-error statements use sample-size limits at fixed gain before gain tends to one; no joint rate is asserted.

Open questions and unproved conjectures

  • The conjectured formula for general degenerate-spectrum unitary orbits.
  • The conjectured local formula for general nonflat fixed-rank spectra.
  • The exact all-density asymptotic minimax value and whether PCT attains it.
