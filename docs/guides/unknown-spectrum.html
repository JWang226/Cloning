Proof guide · chapter 08 of 12

One universal cloner and the compact-set optimum

Paper: Theorem 1.2 · §1.1 · §2.1 · §2.3 · §3.3 · Appendix B

Without the spectrum, the classical Young-label fluctuations must be amplified along with the eigenbasis information. A single spectrum-independent channel achieves the product of the classical and orbital factors. Its minimax optimality needs spectral neighborhoods inside the allowed set.

Where this guide fits in the paper

Reunites both parts of the unknown-spectrum theorem with randomized rounding, compact-uniform attainment, and the full spectral-window converse. Lean uses the concrete universal protocol and proves the dense-interior extension in the full trace-one affine hyperplane; universal-cloner convergence on a singleton does not establish unknown-spectrum minimax optimality there.

At a glance

\lim_{n\to\infty}\sup_{\mathcal M_n}\inf_{p\in K,\,U\in\mathrm U(D)}F\!\left(\mathcal M_n(\rho_{p,U}^{\otimes n}),\rho_{p,U}^{\otimes m_n}\right)=\inf_{p\in K}F_{\mathrm{univ}}(\gamma,p).

Setting and assumptions

  • Physical dimension is D=d+1≥2 for the displayed compact-set converse.
  • Achievability holds on every compact K of positive simple spectra, including singletons.
  • The minimax theorem additionally assumes K is nonempty and closure(interior(toAffine(K)))=toAffine(K) in the full trace-one affine hyperplane.
  • As throughout, m(n)/n→γ>1.
01

Use only the measured label

The universal cloner applies the randomized dilation rule to the measured Young label and then the same physical sector transition as the known-spectrum cloner. The rule is independent of p. Its prescribed version is covariant as a channel identity on all inputs, rather than merely having an invariant payoff on the states being tested.

02

Multiply the two asymptotic costs

The rounded output label law has affinity approaching classicalValue. Uniform lifted factorization multiplies this classical quantity by the orbital sector factor. This proves two-sided convergence of the actual universal cloner’s payoff, uniformly over every p in a compact K and every eigenbasis.

03

Use full local spectral neighborhoods for the converse

At an interior point of K, all sufficiently small trace-zero spectral perturbations stay in K. The full hybrid LAN experiment is therefore available to test every competitor. The Gaussian optimum gives an upper bound by universalValue at that point; the physical theorem constructs the required LAN witness internally.

04

Extend to the boundary and take the worst spectrum

Regular closure makes interior points dense in K. Continuity extends the converse to the infimum of universalValue over all of K. Compact-uniform achievability then supplies the matching minimax lower bound, giving convergence of the actual all-CPTP supremum-infimum.

05

Check a concrete class of admissible sets

The gap-constrained set has smallest eigenvalue and adjacent gaps at least κ. Below κ=2/[D(D+1)] it has an explicit strict interior point and satisfies the theorem’s affine regular-closure condition. At the threshold it is a singleton; above it the constraints are infeasible.

Scope of this step

  • Relative interior in a lower-dimensional spectral subset is insufficient. The interior must be taken in the full D−1 dimensional trace-one hyperplane.
  • For a singleton K, the universal cloner still has its stated fidelity, but a competitor may use the known spectrum and achieve the larger orbital value.
