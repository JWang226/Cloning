Exact rank compression and projector-state optimality

Flat projector states are treated by exact representation identities rather than by taking a singular limit of the simple-spectrum theorem. Rank embeddings compress the physical Cartan channels, identify their fidelity loss, and support an independent finite-dimensional converse.

English explanation · exact Lean excerpts are shown separately.

This chapter in the dependency map →

At a glance

\lim_{n\to\infty}\sup_{\mathcal M_n}\inf_{P\in\mathrm{Gr}(r,D)}F\!\left(\mathcal M_n((P/r)^{\otimes n}),(P/r)^{\otimes m_n}\right)=\gamma^{-r(D-r)/2}.

Setting and assumptions

  • P is a literal Hermitian idempotent of rank r>0 in dimension D=r+k.
  • The nontrivial Grassmann family has 1≤r<D; the formal result also includes the trivial k=0 case.
  • The compression identities allow arbitrary positive supported spectra, and the channel identity applies to every complex input operator.
01

Transport supported states through actual embeddings

Embed the r-dimensional representation into the D-dimensional representation with zero-padded highest weight. The physical Gibbs operator, its trace, and its normalized density commute with zero-padding of the spectrum. This proves conditional support for arbitrary positive rank-r spectra, not only the flat state.

02

Compress the Cartan channel exactly

The constructed Cartan inclusion intertwines the rank embeddings. Inserting this commuting square into the channel formula gives the compression identity for every complex matrix X. Fidelity with a supported target ignores the complementary output block; isometric invariance and homogeneity then give the exact factor sqrt(R_μ/R_ν). In the Lean endpoint the second partition denotes the added weight ω, so the target is μ+ω.

theorem physicalCartanMatrixChannel_rank_supported_compression (mu nu : Fin r→ℕ)
    (hmu : Antitone mu) (hnu : Antitone nu) (k : ℕ)
    (X : Matrix (PartitionIndex mu hmu) (PartitionIndex mu hmu) ℂ) :
    let Ja := rankSectorEmbeddingMatrix mu hmu k
    let Jc := rankSumSectorEmbeddingMatrix mu nu hmu hnu k
    (Jc*Jcᴴ)*((physicalCartanMatrixChannel (padPartition mu k) (padPartition nu k)
        (padPartition_antitone mu hmu k) (padPartition_antitone nu hnu k)).toFun (Ja*X*Jaᴴ))*(Jc*Jcᴴ) =
      rankCartanRatio mu nu hmu hnu k •
        (Jc*(physicalCartanMatrixChannel mu nu hmu hnu).toFun X*Jcᴴ)

theorem nonnegativeCartanGibbsFidelity_padSpectrum

03

Specialize to flat sectors and attain the limit

For the flat rank-r spectrum the internal r-dimensional sector state is maximally mixed. Covariance makes the internal transition exact on that state. The crossing dimension ratios therefore account for the entire sector fidelity; their asymptotics and Young concentration yield γ^(−r(D−r)/2), uniformly over the projector family.

04

Prove a finite converse for all channels

Pinch the output into Schur blocks and average over the unitary orbit. Representation-theoretic projection moments and the weighted fidelity estimate bound every channel by two explicit dimension-ratio moments. This is a finite-sample inequality; no assumed singular LAN model is needed. Surjectivity of the unitary orbit identifies optimization over orbit parameters with optimization over all literal projectors.

05

Match the limit and record the available rate

The finite bound tends to the same value as the constructed channel, proving the Grassmann minimax limit. The upper excess has an O(1/n) bound relative to the finite gain m/n. Replacing m/n by fixed γ at that rate additionally requires m=γn+O(1).
