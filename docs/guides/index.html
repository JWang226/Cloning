Separates the Young concentration estimate at the start of Section 2, character normalization in Appendix A, and local limit in Appendix B into one probabilistic foundation. 03 · From PBW frames to uniform Cartan-sector fidelity

Unpacks the Appendix A proof of the sector-fidelity proposition in Section 2.2 into PBW Gram and frame estimates, Cartan coefficients, and thermal cutoff removal. Lean provides the named quantitative auxiliaries alongside the qualitative Cartan transition limits used by the concrete physical protocols; neither asserts a rate over the whole unbounded Fock basis.

07 · The known-spectrum optimum

Reunites the known-spectrum theorem in Section 1.1 with its exact target-label rule in Section 2.1, attainment in Section 2.3, and converse in Section 3.3. Lean combines concrete sector and probability assembly with the constructed LAN witness; the final optimum is unconditional, although reusable intermediate interfaces remain available.

08 · One universal cloner and the compact-set optimum

Reunites both parts of the unknown-spectrum theorem with randomized rounding, compact-uniform attainment, and the full spectral-window converse. Lean uses the concrete universal protocol and proves the dense-interior extension in the full trace-one affine hyperplane; universal-cloner convergence on a singleton does not establish unknown-spectrum minimax optimality there.

Unpacks the PCT comparison theorem and Section 5 through the Werner and channel preliminaries of Appendix D.1 and the full-rank and projector arguments of D.2–D.3. Lean proves the full-environment limit, the projector limsup comparison, and rank-bound guarantees; it does not replace the projector upper bound by an asserted fixed-gain limit.

11 · Small errors, rank boundaries, and noncommuting limits

Separates the expansions and projector-PCT envelopes in Section 6.3 and Appendix D.3 from the qubit limit-order example discussed in Section 6.4. Lean proves physical target brackets in the stated iterated limits and a concrete qubit obstruction; it does not infer a uniform joint finite-sample rate or prove the general degenerate-spectrum conjectures.

Turns the spectral-order observations in Section 6.1 and the all-state bounds in Section 6.2 into separate monotonicity, exact counterexample, geometric-infimum, and minimax-bound results in Lean. The exact all-density optimum, PCT optimality for that problem, and the general conjectures of Section 6.4 remain open.
