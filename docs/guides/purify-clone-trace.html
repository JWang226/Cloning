Purify–clone–trace: exact channels, limits, and guarantees

PCT is a fixed composition: random purification with a chosen rank bound, Werner cloning in the enlarged space, and partial trace of the purifying register. The proofs evaluate that physical construction and compare it with the sharp cloners, while also giving uniform lower guarantees beyond simple spectra.

English explanation · exact Lean excerpts are shown separately.

This chapter in the dependency map →

Setting and assumptions

  • The rank-bound channel uses a fixed purifying space of dimension r, so the pure-state cloning dimension is rD.
  • The full-rank limit assumes a positive definite density matrix with distinct eigenvalues in D≥2.
  • The projector comparison uses rank r and dimension D=r+k; strict loss requires r>1.
  • The data-processing lower guarantee applies to every density matrix of actual rank at most r, including singular and repeated spectra.
01

Analyze the actual Werner output

The physical Werner channel has an exact occupation-number expansion. Recovering it in Fock coordinates identifies its coefficients and proves trace-norm convergence to a product thermal law. This supplies a state-level limit, not only convergence of finitely many probabilities.

02

Evaluate full-environment PCT

Transport the purification output through the physical construction and trace out the environment. Its limiting spectral and oscillator noise give pctValue, whose radical closed form is proved separately. The prescribed channel handles any initial m≤n by restriction and has the stated physical fidelity limit for every allowed density matrix.

theorem pctValue_closedForm {d : ℕ} {g : ℝ} (hg : 1<g) (p : SimpleSpectrum d) :
    pctValue g p=(Real.sqrt (2*g-1)/g)^(((d : ℝ)-1)/2)*
      ∏ ij : PairIndex d,
        (Real.sqrt (g+(g-1)*p.ratio ij)+Real.sqrt (p.ratio ij*(g-1+g*p.ratio ij)))/
          (g*(1+p.ratio ij))
03

Establish strict asymptotic loss

A scalar comparison shows pctValue<universalValue for γ>1 in dimension at least two. Together with the physical PCT limit and the evaluated universal cloner, this is a comparison between actual channel sequences. On projector states a finite support-factor identity instead yields an upper envelope with an additional classical loss, strictly below the sharp projector value for r>1.

04

Retain a uniform guarantee on all rank-bounded inputs

A purification in dimension rD and monotonicity under partial trace transfer Werner’s finite fidelity lower bound to the same fixed rank-bound PCT channel. The formal endpoint supplies the support and purification facts from the literal matrix-rank condition. The bound gives a certified finite sample ratio and an asymptotic lower bound uniform even for sample-dependent adversarial states.

Scope of this step

  • The projector PCT comparison gives a limsup bound and strict inequality, not existence of a fixed-gain limiting fidelity.
  • A lower guarantee valid on every rank-bounded state does not prove PCT is minimax optimal on that entire class.
