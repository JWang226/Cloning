Young labels: concentration and the local Gaussian law

Schur decomposition separates spectral information in a Young label from the conditional representation state. The formal development identifies the resulting law for actual tensor powers, proves a dimension-uniform tail bound, and resolves its lattice-scale Gaussian behavior.

English explanation · exact Lean excerpts are shown separately.

This chapter in the dependency map →

Setting and assumptions

  • The concentration estimate allows every normalized nonnegative ordered spectrum, including zeros and repetitions.
  • The local L1 and character limits use compact subsets of the positive simple chamber.
  • Lattice densities use their declared interpolation cells and coordinate Jacobian; they are not an unnormalized pointwise approximation.
01

Control the true Young-law tails

An elementary moment-generating-function bound is combined with the finite representation-theoretic prefactor. A sharper finite exponential estimate leaves enough decay to absorb that prefactor after a threshold depending only on dimension. This yields the displayed exp(−N^(1/3)/4) tail estimate, uniformly in the spectrum.

theorem tensorYoungPMF_tail_half_le (p : Fin d→ℝ) (hp : ∀a,0≤p a)

02

Normalize the conditional representation state

The ratio of the highest-weight monomial p^μ to the sector character tends to the product over positive roots of (1−p_j/p_i). The exact orientation of the ratio matters because it is the normalizing constant of the thermal sector limit. A compactness argument upgrades moving-sequence convergence to one threshold for all spectra and all partitions in an arbitrary shrinking window.

theorem eventually_uniform_character_normalization
    (K : Set (SimpleSpectrum d)) (hK : IsCompact K)
    (δ : ℕ→ℝ) (hδ : Tendsto δ atTop (𝓝 0))
    (ε : ℝ) (hε : 0<ε) :
    ∀ᶠ (N : ℕ) in atTop, ∀ p∈K, ∀ μ : Fin d→ℕ, Antitone μ →
      (∀ i, |(μ i:ℝ)/(N:ℝ)-p.eigenvalue i|≤δ N) →
      |(∏ i, p.eigenvalue i ^ μ i)/physicalSectorCharacter μ p.eigenvalue -
        spectralCorrection p.eigenvalue|<ε
03

Pass from lattice mass to density

The interpolated physical Young law converges in L1 to the Gaussian on the trace-zero spectral hyperplane. Local estimates control the central cells, while concentration controls the tails. Compactness makes the error uniform over the allowed spectra, with covariance evaluated at the current spectrum.

theorem uniform_tensorYoungDensity_l1 (d : ℕ) (K : Set (Fin (d + 1) → ℝ))
    (hK : IsCompact K) (hp : ∀ p ∈ K, ∑ i, p i = 1)
    (hp0 : ∀ p ∈ K, ∀ i, 0 < p i) (hord : ∀ p ∈ K, StrictAnti p)
    (ε : ℝ) (hε : 0 < ε) :
    ∃ N₀ : ℕ, ∀ N ≥ N₀, ∀ (p : Fin (d + 1) → ℝ) (hpK : p ∈ K),
      (∫ x, |tensorYoungDensity d N p (fun i ↦ (hp0 p hpK i).le) (hp p hpK) x -
        covarianceGaussian d p (hp p hpK) x| ∂volume) < ε

Scope of this step

  • Uniform local Gaussian limits here do not cover a family approaching eigenvalue collisions or zero eigenvalues.
  • Concentration is substantially more general than the simple-spectrum local limit; their hypotheses should not be conflated.
